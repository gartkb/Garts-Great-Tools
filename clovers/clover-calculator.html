Where the gold goes

Forge rates: Shiny & Selici's Mystic Clover data (14,000 single forges). Recipe, vendor costs and weekly limits: Guild Wars 2 Wiki. Prices: official GW2 API. Weekly purchase limits on conversion items are not tracked; pick the conversion you'd actually use.

Single forge: 1 Mystic Coin, 1 Glob of Ectoplasm, 1 Obsidian Shard, 6 Philosopher's Stones. On a miss it returns one of: 2–4 of a T6 fine material (40%), 1–3 of a T6 refined material (20%), 1 T5 lodestone or Putrid Essence (4%), 5 Mystic Coins, 2 Obsidian Shards, 2 Ectoplasm, 2 Mystic Crystals (about 1% each), or a Hidden Treasure.

Encryption drop rates: Zanar, Selici & Shinymeta's Fractal Encryption data (1,000,000 encryptions, with the Recursive Resourcing mastery trained). Ascended recipes and Bags of Rare Gear can also drop; they're account bound or negligible and count as 0.
